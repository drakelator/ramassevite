Frequently Asked Questions

How does pricing work?

Our prices are based on the volume your items take up in our truck. The rate includes labor, transport, and recycling fees. No hidden costs!

Which areas do you serve?

We cover the entire South Shore of Montreal (Longueuil, Brossard, Boucherville, etc.) as well as the island of Montreal and surroundings.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Ideally, small loose items should be boxed, but it's not mandatory. Our team picks up everything, wherever it is (basement, attic, garage).

What happens to the junk?

We prioritize recycling and donation. Usable items are donated to local charities, and the rest is sorted at certified centers.
