Our Mission

At Ramasse Vite, our mission is simple: to simplify people's lives while reducing the ecological footprint. We believe that getting rid of old items shouldn't be a headache, nor a source of pollution.

We meticulously sort every load to maximize recycling and donation to local organizations on the South Shore and Montreal.

Why Choose Us?

  • Speed: Same-day or next-day service.
  • Transparency: Clear pricing based on volume.
  • Ecology: Priority on recycling and donation.
  • Local: A local company for local people.
