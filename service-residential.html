Frequently Asked Questions

Do you pick up from inside?

Yes! Unlike municipal pickups, we come inside your home. Basement, attic, garage, upstairs... we go where the items are.

Do you take old mattresses?

Absolutely. Mattresses are very common items that we collect and recycle.

How does pricing work?

The price is based on the volume your items occupy in our truck. It is an all-inclusive price (labor, transport, recycling).
